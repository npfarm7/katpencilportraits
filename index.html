About the Artist

Katia Chalin

Every portrait begins with a story — a face you love, a moment you want to hold onto, a gift that means something. I work in pencil because of the quiet precision it allows: every line considered, every shadow drawn by hand.

Whether it's a portrait of a loved one, a beloved pet, or a meaningful moment captured forever in graphite, each piece is created with care from your reference photos and finished as a one-of-a-kind heirloom.

Commissions are open. I'd love to hear about the portrait you have in mind.
